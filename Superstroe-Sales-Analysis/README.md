#  Superstore Sales Analysis

##  Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze sales and profitability data from a retail superstore to identify trends, uncover loss-making areas, and improve business performance.

---

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables & Charts
- Data Cleaning & Transformation
- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

---

##  Dataset Description

The dataset includes:
- Order Date
- Region
- Category & Sub-category
- Sales & Profit
- Discount

---

##  Approach (CRISP-DM Methodology)

1. **Business Understanding**
   - Understand sales performance and profitability

2. **Data Understanding**
   - Explore trends across regions, categories, and time

3. **Data Preparation**
   - Cleaned and structured dataset for analysis

4. **Analysis**
   - Created KPIs and visual summaries

5. **Evaluation**
   - Identified profit drivers and loss-making segments

---

## Key Insights

-  High sales do NOT always mean high profit
-  Heavy discounts significantly reduce profitability
-  Certain regions generate consistent losses
-  Some product categories perform well in sales but poorly in profit
-  Sub-categories like furniture often have low margins

---

##  Business Recommendations

- Optimize discount strategy to protect margins
- Focus on high-profit products instead of just high sales
- Re-evaluate pricing strategy for low-margin categories
- Improve regional strategies where losses are frequent
